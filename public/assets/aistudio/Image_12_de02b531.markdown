## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-echelon Vedic astrology consulting, merging centuries-old sacred cosmic wisdom with contemporary luxury editorial aesthetics. It positions spiritual consultation not as mystic novelty, but as an elevated, bespoke advisory discipline for discerning clients seeking clarity, purpose, and karmic alignment.

The aesthetic blends **Dark Editorial Modernism** with **Ethereal Tactile Luxury**:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep cosmic voids balanced by warm parchment illumination, evoking royal observatories, star charts, and sacred ancient palm-leaf manuscripts preserved in gilded vaults.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Quiet reverence, profound credibility, timeless authority, and transcendent calm.
- **Visual Motif:** Fine golden celestial coordinates, delicate geometric mandalas, subtle starlight radials, and precision hair-thin borders that balance mysticism with executive polish.

## Colors

The palette relies on deliberate contrast between absolute cosmic depths and radiant celestial metallics:

- **Primary (`#D4AF37` - Celestial Gold):** Reserved for focal calls-to-action, primary icons, auspicious chart markers, active indicators, and delicate borders. Represents solar energy, spiritual sovereignty, and karmic clarity.
- **Secondary (`#7C3AED` - Royal Cosmic Violet):** Employed for ambient starlight glows, secondary accents, spiritual tags, and ethereal depth highlights. Represents intuitive wisdom, the crown chakra, and mystical insight.
- **Tertiary (`#FDFBF7` - Warm Parchment):** Used for elevated text, refined surface overlays, high-status badges, and contemplative pull-quotes. Provides organic warmth against cool obsidian space.
- **Neutral (`#0B0F19` - Deep Midnight Obsidian):** The foundational cosmic canvas. Secondary surface tones transition into `#111827` (Nocturne Slate) and `#1A2234` (Astral Charcoal) for dimensional layering.

Functional semantics maintain high legibility:
- Success: `#10B981` (Emerald / Mercury harmony)
- Critical / Warning: `#F59E0B` (Amber / Mars radiance)
- Text Primary: `#FDFBF7` (Parchment White)
- Text Secondary: `#9CA3AF` (Stardust Grey)

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy strikes a deliberate chord between classical, ceremonial authority and contemporary functional clarity:

- **Playfair Display (Headlines & Display):** Conveys the aristocratic provenance of ancient Vedic wisdom and royal horoscope lineage. Used for main landing declarations, consultation tier titles, quote pullouts, and astrological chart headings. Italic styling is reserved for philosophical epigraphs and Sanskrit translation notes.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body & Labels):** Delivers clean optical tracking, open apertures, and razor-sharp readability for birth-chart breakdowns, planetary transit reports, booking details, and long-form advisory copy.
- **Editorial Micro-treatments:** All labels, badges, and astrological metadata (e.g., Nakshatra, Lagna, Dasha periods) utilize uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.08em` to `0.12em`) to mirror luxury horology and antique celestial cartography.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a fluid 12-column grid anchored by generous negative space, creating a serene, unhurried browsing journey:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with 80px to 120px max horizontal margins. Structural sections breathe with vast vertical rhythm (`80px` to `128px` between narrative blocks) to evoke cosmic vastness.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 32px margins and 24px gutters. Secondary sidebars collapse into unified horizontal tab rails.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with 20px edge margins and 16px gutters. Sacred geometry diagrams and birth chart calculators adapt to 100% viewport width card containers.
- **Component Spacing:** Component internal padding uses strict multiples of `0.25rem` (4px). Primary interactive cards maintain `24px` to `32px` interior padding to preserve an airy, premium aura.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on harsh Drop Shadows; instead, it is manifested through **Starlight Luminescence, Tonal Translucency, and Gilded Boundaries**:

- **Canvas Foundation:** Layer 0 (`#0B0F19`) provides an infinite dark backdrop, often textured with faint vector constellation lines (opacity 4% to 8%).
- **Elevated Glass Surfaces (Tier 1):** Background `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.72)` combined with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a 1px border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`.
- **Active & Focal Elements (Tier 2):** Background `rgba(26, 34, 52, 0.85)` with ambient celestial glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 35px -5px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12), 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Mystic Glows:** Radial overlays utilizing royal violet (`rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.08)`) and warm solar gold (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.06)`) generate subtle depth spotlights behind profile imagery and astrological chart wheels.

## Shapes

The geometric vernacular balances modern architectural restraint with the sacred precision of Vedic yantras:

- **Corner Radii:** Strict `roundedness: 1` standardizes subtle 4px (`rounded-sm`) to 8px (`rounded-md`) corners on cards, interactive buttons, inputs, and modals. Harsh industrial squareness is softened, while overly playful pill shapes are avoided to preserve spiritual gravitas.
- **Geometric Accents:** Clean 45-degree chamfers, hairline intersecting golden axes, and concentric circular enclosures represent celestial orbits, astrolabes, and the Navamsha chart structure.
- **Framing Lines:** Enclosures frequently showcase recessed corner ticks or delicate double-line golden headers (1px solid with 2px gap) referencing royal decrees and antique Vedic horoscopes.

## Components

### Buttons & Call-to-Actions
- **Primary CTA ("Reserve Consultation"):** Solid celestial gold (`#D4AF37`) fill with deep obsidian text (`#0B0F19`), font weight 600, uppercase label tracking. Hover state triggers a subtle starlight bloom: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.45)`.
- **Secondary CTA ("Explore Vedic Services"):** Ghost style with 1px border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`, warm parchment text (`#FDFBF7`). Hover state fills background with `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)` and brightens the border to 100% gold opacity.
- **Tertiary / Karmic Link:** Playfair Display italic link flanked by delicate celestial dash arrows (`— →`) in gold.

### Consultation Cards & Pricing Tiers
- **Structure:** Surface background in `#111827` at 85% opacity, framed in 1px `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.16)`. 
- **Featured / Recommended Tier:** Accented with an ornate top border trim in solid `#D4AF37` and an illuminated astral violet halo behind the pricing banner.
- **Content:** Includes session duration, birth-chart calculation scope, planetary remediation guidance, and bespoke PDF dossier inclusions.

### Input Fields & Birth-Data Selectors
- **Form Elements:** Minimal translucent fields with `rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.6)` background, parchment white typography, and hairline `rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.15)` borders.
- **Focus State:** Transition to border color `#D4AF37` with an ambient glow (`0 0 12px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`).
- **Specialized Inputs:** Time-of-birth precision dial, coordinate geocoder for exact birth latitude/longitude, and Vedic moon sign (Rashi) / Nakshatra quick-finders.

### Chips & Badges
- **Vedic Credibility Chips:** Low-profile capsules with subtle cosmic borders (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`), containing miniature astrological icons (Sun, Jupiter, Ascendant) alongside uppercase label typography (`label-sm`).
- **Parchment Verified Badges:** Used for trust metrics ("30+ Years Heritage", "5,000+ Natal Charts Analyzed", "Gold Medalist Vedic Astrologer").

### Testimonials & Client Endorsements
- **Quote Cards:** Subtle glass cards highlighting client life transformations (corporate executives, public figures, families). Embellished with delicate gold quotation marks in Playfair Display and five-point star constellation ratings.

### Specialized Vedic Widgets
- **Interactive Kundli (Birth Chart) Wheel:** Dynamic SVG chart with gold coordinate strokes, violet planet glyphs (Grahas), and translucent house sectors.
- **Panchang Quick-Bar:** Sticky header indicator tracking current Tithi, Nakshatra, and Rahu Kaal auspicious timings.
