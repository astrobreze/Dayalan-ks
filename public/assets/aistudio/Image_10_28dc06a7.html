Frequently Asked Questions on Kundli Matching

Essential clarity on Guna points, Rajju checks, and KP methodology.

A score between 18 and 24 represents an "acceptable to average" match in classical Vedic terms. In such charts, the longevity of the bond relies heavily on the presence of Rajju Porutham, a fortified 7th House Jupiter aspect, and favorable planetary Dasha periods. When the core points like Nadi and Bhakoot are unhindered, marriage proceeds happily with minor customized planetary remediation.
Traditional Parashari astrology flags Mars in houses 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, or 12 as severe Manglik Dosha. However, Astrologer Dhayalan K S applies KP (Krishnamurti Padhdhati) stellar astrology to examine the 7th Cusp Sub-Lord. If the 7th sub-lord connects favorably with houses 2, 7, and 11 and does not link with 6 or 12, the Manglik Dosha remains inert and poses no threat to the marital longevity.
In Tamil and South Indian astrology, Rajju is deemed the sovereign of all Poruthams. Nakshatras are mapped to five parts of the celestial body: Shiro (Head), Kanta (Neck), Udhara (Stomach), Kati (Waist), and Pada (Feet). If the groom and bride share identical Rajju limbs (especially Shiro or Kanta), it represents a severe dosha threatening marital longevity. A positive Rajju match guarantees protection, stability, and enduring vitality.
Dasa Sandhi occurs when both partners conclude a major Mahadasha or change major ruling planets within an overlapping timeframe of 6 to 12 months. This simultaneous karmic shift can introduce turbulent transitions, financial reconfiguration, or relocations.
